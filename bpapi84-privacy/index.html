Creator OS Privacy Policy

Effective: October 19, 2026

Creator OS is a personal creator tool operated by Robert Matlock. This policy explains how data from connected accounts is handled.

TikTok data we access

  • Basic profile information (display name, avatar, open ID)
  • Account statistics (followers, following, likes, video count)
  • Your list of public videos and their metrics
  • Permission to upload your own videos to your TikTok drafts inbox

How it is used

This data is used only to show the account owner their own stats and to upload their own content as drafts. Creator OS does not access other users' data.

Storage & security

Access tokens and data are stored securely and kept only as long as needed to provide the service.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties.

Revoking access

You can revoke Creator OS's access at any time in your TikTok app settings under Security & permissions → Apps and services permissions.

Deletion

To have your data deleted, email creatorosadmin@gmail.com and it will be removed promptly.
